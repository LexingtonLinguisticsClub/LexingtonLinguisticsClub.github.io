About Us

Community Focused

We are a community of language enthusiasts based in Lexington, Massachusetts. Whether you're new to linguistics or a seasoned researcher, our club offers a welcoming environment for all.

Our Goals

Promote Linguistic Awareness

Increase understanding and appreciation of language diversity in our community.

Educational Opportunities

Provide workshops, seminars, and discussions on various linguistic topics.

Computational Linguistics

Explore natural language processing, language modeling, and more.

Community Building

Create a network of individuals passionate about languages and linguistics.
